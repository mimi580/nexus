Controls

Emergency stop halts all agent work until you press Resume. The background worker also runs passes on its own schedule.

New objective

Approve sends the draft exactly as shown in the edit box (all safety checks still apply). Reject closes the deal and the action is never proposed again. Resolve means you handled it yourself.

Find suppliers now

NEXUS also searches weekly, and immediately whenever a deal is waiting for a supplier offer. Qualified suppliers receive an RFQ built from your buyers' demand; their quotes arrive in Reviews for you to check before they enter the catalogue.

Advertising

NEXUS plans Google search and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) campaigns per product line and country from your catalogue, writes and checks the ads, and sends people to a landing page with a quotation form. Pharmaceuticals are never advertised. New campaigns wait in Reviews for your approval; after launch NEXUS moves budget toward what brings enquiries, replaces losing ads and pauses campaigns that spend without results.

Product photos for Meta ads

Real photos of the stock convert far better than generated cards. JPG or PNG, at least 500×500. Used in rotation for that product line.

Landing pages

One page per product line and country, written only from your catalogue, price book and licences. Views, WhatsApp clicks and enquiries are counted per page.

What NEXUS has learned

Each repeated choice is tested against real results: e-mail angles and subject styles (positive replies), ad angles (enquiries per click), supplier regions (quotes received) and the opportunity scoring weights (which contacted deals engaged). "Chance best" is how likely each option is to be the best given the data so far; options with little data still get tried.

Import from CSV

Offers are real supplier quotes; prices are what buyers pay. Every row needs a source. A file with any bad row imports nothing and lists the problems.
